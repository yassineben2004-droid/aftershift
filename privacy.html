Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit or buy from AfterShift, why, and what your rights are.

What we collect

  • Order data: name, email, phone number (optional) and shipping address you enter at checkout.
  • Payment data: handled directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous, aggregated visit data via Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

Why we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, and to send you order and tracking emails (performance of the contract).
  • To meet legal and tax obligations and to prevent fraud (legal obligation and legitimate interest).

We don't sell your data and we don't send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need to run the store: Stripe (payments), our suppliers and couriers (to deliver your order), Google (our email provider) and Vercel (website hosting). Some of these providers may process data outside your country, under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

Order data is kept for as long as required by tax and accounting rules, then deleted.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or profiling cookies. The cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it isn't lost between pages; Stripe may set technical cookies needed to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to its processing, by emailing support@aftershiftstore.shop. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; California residents have the rights granted by the CCPA.
