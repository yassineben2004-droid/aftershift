Shipping & Returns

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: free worldwide shipping · processing in 1-2 business days · delivery in 7-12 business days · tracking number by email · 14 days to return.

Shipping

We ship worldwide, free of charge, to every country listed at checkout. Orders are processed within 1-2 business days; delivery then takes 7-12 business days depending on your country. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking number. During peak periods (Black Friday, Christmas) delivery may take a few days longer: if your order is a gift, please order early.

Customs and import duties. For some countries outside the US, the courier or your local customs office may charge import duties or taxes on delivery. These are set by your country, are not included in our prices, and are paid by the recipient.

Make sure your address is complete and correct: if a parcel is returned to us because of a wrong or incomplete address, we'll contact you to arrange a new shipment.

Returns (14 days)

You can return your order within 14 days of delivery, for any reason. To start a return:

  1. Email us at aftershift.store@gmail.com with your order number (it starts with AS-).
  2. We reply with the return address and instructions.
  3. Ship the item back in its original condition, unused and complete with all parts. Return shipping costs are paid by the customer.

Once we receive and check the item, we refund the full price you paid to your original payment method within 14 days. For hygiene reasons, please don't return products that have been worn and used.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these terms include your statutory right of withdrawal within 14 days of delivery.

Damaged, faulty or wrong item

If your item arrives damaged, doesn't work, or isn't what you ordered, email us as soon as you notice with your order number and a photo or short video. After checking, we send you a replacement or refund you in full — and in this case we cover the return shipping.
